Frequently asked questions

How do I add a drive?

Plug it in, click “Add a drive” in Disk Finder on your Mac and allow access once. The drive is read, and its contents stay browsable even when it's unplugged. NAS shares are added with “Add a NAS”.

Does the catalog update by itself?

Yes. While Disk Finder is open or in the menu bar, every plugged-in drive is followed live: files added, changed or deleted. NAS shares are re-read on the schedule you choose.

What about iPhone and iPad?

The same catalog arrives through iCloud, with the same Apple Account. You can search from anywhere, and even scan a drive connected over USB-C or a network share opened in Files.

One purchase for all my devices?

Yes: a single purchase, no subscription, for Mac, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account. On another device, Disk Finder installs from the App Store without paying again.

How do teams work?

A team is a library of drives shared between several people, each on their own Mac. Its creator shares a code and a password; everyone sees the team's drives and who added them. Your “Personal” library stays private.

What are render files?

Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve create renders, proxies and caches they can rebuild on their own. Disk Finder finds them and can delete them to free up space. Your source files, edits and titles are never touched.

Spotlight

Files on your drives, even unplugged, show up in your Mac's ⌘Space search, with the drive they're on. You can turn this off in Settings.

Is my data safe?

Disk Finder collects nothing. Your catalog stays on your devices and in your private iCloud. Privacy policy.
