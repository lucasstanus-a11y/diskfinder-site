Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

In short: Disk Finder collects no data. No account to create, no ads, no trackers, no usage analytics. The developer has no access to your catalog or your files.

What the app does

Disk Finder reads the names, sizes and dates of the files and folders on the drives you add, to build a catalog you can browse even when a drive is unplugged. It does not read the contents of your files.

Where your data goes

  • On your device: the catalog is stored on your Mac, iPhone or iPad.
  • In your private iCloud: so the same catalog appears on all your devices, it syncs through iCloud with your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it.
  • With your team, if you create or join one: drives you add to a team are shared, through iCloud, with that team's members only. Your first name, or the name you choose, is shown to them. The invitation code is stored encrypted in iCloud; it cannot be read without the team's password.
  • In Spotlight: if the feature is on, your file names are added to your Mac's Spotlight index, which stays on that Mac.

Purchase

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. Disk Finder only checks with Apple that the app was purchased; it receives no payment information.

If you write to us

If you email us, your address and message are used only to reply. They are never sold or used for advertising.

Children

Disk Finder collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
